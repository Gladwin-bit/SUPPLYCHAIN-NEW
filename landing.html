Process

From Loom to Last Mile

Every Kasaragod saree follows a cryptographically secured journey, verifiable by anyone at any time.

01
🧵
Weaver Registers

Manufacturer mints the saree on-chain with loom location, weave date, scratch-off secret hash & certificate.

02
✅
Cooperative Verifies

Registered cooperative inspects and co-signs the product, certifying physical and quality authenticity.

03
🚚
Distributor Transfers

Custody passes via cryptographic handover keys — no key, no transfer. Counterfeit distribution impossible.

04
🏪
Shop Receives

Retail shop claims custody and marks the product AtShop, visible in the immutable blockchain ledger.

05
📱
Customer Verifies

Buyer scans QR, enters scratch-off code. One-time claim marks the saree Sold — cloned codes never work twice.

Features

Built for Trust

Every layer of KasChain is engineered to make fraud structurally impossible.

🔐

Role-Based Access Control

Only manufacturers can mint. Only cooperatives can verify. Smart contract enforces every rule on-chain.

🔑

Rolling Handover Keys

Each custody transfer requires a secret key. The key rotates on every transfer — replay attacks are impossible.

🏷️

One-Time Scratch-Off Claim

Consumer secret hashes are stored on-chain. Once claimed, the code is permanently consumed.

📦

Batch & Single Product Support

Register individual sarees or bulk batches. Each product gets a unique on-chain identity (A1, A2, B1…).
